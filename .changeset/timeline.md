Add `Timeline`, a read-only sequence of events along a horizontal or vertical
line (Takeoff Core `tk-timeline`). Compose `Timeline.Item` with
`Timeline.Title`, `Timeline.Description` and `Timeline.Time`; `placement`
(`alternate` | `start` | `end`) picks the side of the line, and `indicator`
swaps the dot for an icon.

`@takeoff-design/tokens` ships the `tk-timeline-*` recipe.
